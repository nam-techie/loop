# LOOP Brand Rules

## Color system

Use only the approved brand colors below. Do not introduce alternate greens, oranges, creams, grays, or accent colors.

- **Forest Voltage** — `#002901` / RGB `0, 41, 1`
  - Primary background, dark surfaces, navigation, footer, and large color fields.
- **Blaze Punch** — `#FD3E00` / RGB `253, 62, 0`
  - Primary accent, active states, links, buttons, highlights, borders, and emphasis.
- **Shadow Whisper** — `#010101` / RGB `1, 1, 1`
  - Use only for the deepest contrast, image overlays, and fine structural contrast.
- **Warm Off-White** — `#F2F0E8` / RGB `242, 240, 232`
  - Use sparingly where the green/orange combination becomes too heavy or unreadable. This is the only light color allowed.

### Color rules

1. Green and orange are the core palette and should carry the visual identity.
2. Prefer green backgrounds with orange accents.
3. Use warm off-white only to relieve dense areas or support legibility; never add beige, gray, or another white.
4. Do not use gradients that introduce colors outside this palette.
5. Images may retain their natural colors, but all interface surfaces, type, borders, and controls must follow this palette.

## Typography

- **Display font:** Alega only. Use for the LOOP wordmark, large headings, campaign statements, and expressive navigation labels.
- **Body font:** Roboto only. Use for descriptions, product details, utility labels, forms, FAQs, and supporting text.
- Keep display typography bold, condensed-looking, and editorial. Do not substitute another display style when the Alega font is available.
- Use uppercase labels with generous tracking for navigation and metadata.

## Interface direction

- Keep the composition editorial, product-led, and spacious.
- Use strong asymmetry, oversized display type, thin structural rules, and photography from the forest/product campaign.
- Preserve the five-page structure: Home, Collections, About, Contact, and Cart.
